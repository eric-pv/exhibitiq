Theissen Training Systems (TTS) has been at the forefront of developing and manufacturing sophisticated live fire training systems for over three decades. Renowned as a leader in the industry, we specialize in designing and implementing comprehensive training solutions for military and law enforcement applications. Our extensive portfolio includes everything from Small Arms Ranges to Military Operations in Urban Terrain (MOT) facilities and advanced Combined Arms Live-Fire Exercise (CALFEX) Ranges that support complex tank and attack helicopter operations. At TTS, we are committed to enhancing the tactical readiness of armed forces and law enforcement agencies worldwide.

Modular Target Systems And Customized Range Equipment

We design, manufacture, and install comprehensive training range systems and live fire targets tailored for law enforcement and military professionals. Our modular product range allows us to integrate proven components from our existing offerings to create customized solutions that meet our clients' specific needs. We are committed to delivering high-quality, adaptable training equipment that enhances operational readiness and performance.

Infantry Targets

We provide various multifunctional target mechanisms suitable for all infantry shooting ranges. Our offerings include Stationary Infantry Targets (SIT) and Moving Infantry Targets (MIT), designed to enhance training in marksmanship, Military Operations in Urbanized Terrain (MOUT), and tactical live-fire operations. These versatile targets support comprehensive training needs, ensuring preparedness in various combat scenarios.

Armor Targets

We provide an extensive selection of Stationary Armor Targets (SAT) in full and half-size options, featuring flank or head-on views. Additionally, our Moving Armor Target (MAT) lineup includes various dynamic systems, such as self-propelled or cable-pulled mechanisms that move silhouettes at high speeds. These modular and portable systems can accommodate curved trails, ensuring flexibility and adaptability for comprehensive armored vehicle training scenarios.

Turning Target Systems

We provide turning targets that independently rotate target silhouettes, offering dynamic and adaptable training for both indoor and outdoor ranges. These targets are designed to meet the rigorous demands of military and law enforcement professionals. Available in various installation modes, our turning targets ensure flexibility and effectiveness across multiple training scenarios.

Target Retrieval System

We provide target retrieval systems that are ideally suited for indoor shooting ranges' dynamic and scalable demands, catering to military and civilian government special operations forces. These target systems are meticulously designed to accommodate training across various shooting distances and feature mechanisms capable of presenting targets in multiple orientations.

Electronic Target Scoring Systems

We provide advanced electronic scoring targets that are essential for enhancing precision shooting. Our advanced LOMAH (Location-of-Miss-And-Hit) system delivers precise feedback on shot placement for supersonic projectiles. Our Box Targets technology offers accurate scoring and marking capabilities for subsonic projectiles. These systems are crucial for real-time adjustments in training, competition, or personal skill development, helping shooters effectively refine their techniques.

Deployable Ranges

We provide deployable range systems that are perfect for remote training and suitable for home base and out-of-area locations. Understanding the difficulties of conducting live-fire exercises in unfamiliar territories, where local equipment might not be available or compatible, our systems are specifically designed for ease of use. These deployable range systems are lightweight, simple to install for temporary setups, and designed for compact storage and transport in standard containers, ensuring they meet the logistical and operational needs of dynamic training environments.
