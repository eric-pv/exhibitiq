Virtual Reality Training Systems for Law Enforcement and Military

Operator XR is built by operators, for operators. Our team is made up of former special forces, police and other law enforcement personnel. Every system we build reflects real front-line operational and training experience.

The result is a totally immersive 3D virtual reality training simulator for police, law enforcement, military and other public safety & security agencies. Trusted by over 120 customers worldwide, and delivered through Operator XR's OP-2 System.

Why Choose Virtual Reality Training with OP-2

Train more often, more effectively, for a fraction of the cost of live-fire and range time.

VR triggers the same adrenaline and stress response as the real thing, so the first time it counts won't be the first time they've faced it.

Repeat any scenario as many times as it takes. Instructor-led review after every session builds muscle memory and faster decisions.

VR training boosts information retention and builds confidence and preparedness for real-world scenarios.

Core Capabilities

Weapon integration

Train with real-world replica weapons.

  • Support for multiple tracked weapons and tools per trainee including OC spray, CEW and flashlight.

Scenario control

Fully customizable scenarios from thousands of pre-built assets.

  • For law enforcement: active shooter, use-of-force decision-making, de-escalation, crisis intervention, hostage rescue and SWAT.
  • For military and tactical teams: CQB, counter drone (C-sUAS) tactics and more.

After Action Review

After Action Review from multiple angles, with full training metrics. Shot placement, eye tracking, reaction times, draw times and more.

  • Every action and voice is recorded and can be exported to an external hard drive.
  • Instructors control suspect actions, dialogue, and scenario timing throughout, so a session runs its full course.

Multi-user realism

Direct every scenario live. Headset view, suspect view, role-player view, top-down, or elevated room cameras.

  • Voice suspects in real time over a networked mic, with trainees never removing their headsets.
  • Bookmark key moments for instant recall, backed by a secure, locally stored analytics system that tracks each trainee's progress over time.

Space & setup

Up to 10,000 sq ft of trackable training space.

  • The full system packs into a single case and is ready to train in under 30 minutes.
  • No backpack computers, lighthouses, link boxes, or dedicated IT department.
  • Instructors create and run every scenario from a tablet alone.
  • Secure offline updates are available with no cloud connectivity required.
