Our Story

About Ti Training

Established in 2006 as a fully owned subsidiary of Table Mountain Group LLC, Ti Training has become a renowned supplier of immersive training solutions catering to law enforcement, military personnel, and the broader public.

With a satisfied clientele exceeding 1,200 globally, Ti Training is recognized for providing top-notch products, dependable support, and outstanding simulation training experiences.

Situated in Golden, Colorado, our Corporate Training and Production Facility encompasses offices, production spaces, an award-winning video studio, research and development facilities, and a comprehensive training center specializing in Ti Training, simulation training, and RECON Training.

At Ti Training, we don’t just keep up with the latest trends – we set them!
