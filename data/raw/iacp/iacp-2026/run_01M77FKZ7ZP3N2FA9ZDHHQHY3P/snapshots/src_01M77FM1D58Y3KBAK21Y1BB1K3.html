COUNTER-UAS DETECTION

KNOW WHAT'S IN YOUR AIRSPACE

Rapid Drone doesn't just put aircraft overhead — we detect the ones that shouldn't be there.
Unauthorized drones are a growing threat over events, facilities, borders, and sensitive sites. Rapid Drone provides passive airspace awareness — detecting, tracking, and identifying drone activity so your team knows what’s flying, where, and when, in time to respond. Our detection layer is passive and non-emitting: it listens rather than transmits, so it raises no spectrum or interference concerns and integrates cleanly alongside ongoing operations.

STATE-OF-THE-ART · ACOUSTIC

ACOUSTIC DRONE DETECTION

Purpose-built acoustic sensing identifies and tracks drones by their distinct sound signature — no radar, no radio emissions, no active transmission. It works where other methods struggle: against drones flying low, slow, dark, or in radio-quiet modes that defeat RF-only systems.
  • Passive & non-emitting — listens only, no spectrum use or interference
  • Detects RF-silent threats — catches autonomous and radio-quiet drones
  • Real-time alerting — detection, tracking, and identification feeding your team
  • Site-flexible — fixed installations or rapid deployment for events and temporary perimeters

FREQUENTLY ASKED

QUESTIONS AGENCIES ASK

Is Rapid Drone's equipment NDAA-compliant?
Yes. Our fleet is built on a U.S.-controlled, NDAA-compliant supply chain. The multirotor platforms we field are Blue UAS Cleared, and the Pathfinder-X is built on a U.S.-controlled supply chain with no foreign-controlled hardware and no FCC Covered List exposure. We’re happy to walk procurement teams through specifics for their requirements.
Do you sell drones, or operate them?
We’re a drone services and intelligence provider — not a manufacturer. We design, operate, and manage end-to-end programs, from flight operations and data capture through analysis and integration. We also offer managed-service models so agencies can field capability without building an in-house program from scratch.
Are your pilots FAA-certified?
Yes. Our pilots are FAA-certified and operate in strict adherence to all federal, state, and local regulations. Flight safety and regulatory compliance are led by our Chief of Flight Safety, a veteran aviator with decades of operational and check-airman experience.
What does the Pathfinder-X actually do that other drones don't?

It pairs vertical launch and recovery with fixed-wing range and endurance, and the Starling Hub ground station performs autonomous battery swaps to keep an asset on station with minimal crew burden and no rotation gap. That means persistent, wide-area coverage across distances and terrain that rotary platforms and ground units can’t reach in time.

How is mission data handled and secured?
Our detection and operations layer is built around secure data handling and transmission, designed to meet agency and federal procurement requirements. We tailor data-handling, retention, and integration to each agency’s policies and command-center environment.
What markets do you serve?

Law enforcement, state agencies, border and perimeter security, defense and federal support, critical infrastructure and utilities, and executive and personal protection — plus passive counter-UAS acoustic detection. We match the platform to the mission across each.
